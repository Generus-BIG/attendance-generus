# LUPG Reporting

The LUPG reporting context captures monthly collective progress and evidence for each kelompok while keeping repetitive reporting practical for team managers.

## Language

**Jenjang**:
A stage of age-based development used in LUPG reports: `ACR`, `APR`, `AR`, or `GPN`.
_Avoid_: category, assessment category

**Konteks penerapan**:
An environment in which character application is assessed, such as family, school or study place, community, congregation, or workplace. Available contexts may differ by jenjang.
_Avoid_: character, assessment item

**Penilaian penerapan karakter**:
A monthly collective assessment of 29-character application for one **jenjang × konteks penerapan** combination. It is neither an individual score nor a separate assessment of each of the 29 characters.
_Avoid_: individual character score, percentage

**Tingkat penerapan karakter**:
One of five ordered assessments: **Perlu pembinaan**, **Belum diterapkan**, **Mulai diterapkan**, **Mulai konsisten**, or **Sudah menjadi kebiasaan**.
_Avoid_: progress percentage

**Belum dinilai**:
The absence of an assessment for a report row. It is represented separately from **Belum diterapkan** and is not an assessment level.
_Avoid_: Belum diterapkan, Belum terpantau

**Perlu pembinaan**:
The lowest character-application assessment, indicating that coaching is needed. It must include a short note explaining the coaching context.
_Avoid_: Perlu dimusyawarahkan
