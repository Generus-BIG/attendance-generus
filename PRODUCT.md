# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MuMiBig administrators review, validate, and present operational and monthly reporting data, primarily during focused desktop sessions. Team Managers enter attendance and LUPG reporting data for their own kelompok, often on a phone between community activities, with limited time, one-handed touch input, and occasionally unreliable connectivity.

## Product Purpose

The product replaces fragmented attendance and LUPG reporting workflows with one dependable administrative workspace. It helps users record participants and attendance, complete monthly reports, monitor program and character outcomes, review village-level recaps, and present trustworthy results. Success means Team Managers can complete repetitive reporting quickly and accurately while administrators can review the same data without manual reconciliation.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, considered, reliable. The interface should feel neutral and workmanlike, with clarity and typographic hierarchy carrying the experience. It should disappear into the task rather than call attention to itself.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Bootstrap administration templates, colored accent stripes, excessive badges, crypto or AI dashboard aesthetics, neon gradients, glassmorphism, over-branded SaaS chrome, decorative motion, and warm editorial styling that competes with operational data.

## Design Principles

1. Make frequent operational tasks faster than their paper or spreadsheet equivalents.
2. Preserve one clear information hierarchy across desktop review, mobile entry, and presentation contexts.
3. Prefer progressive disclosure over long, exhausting forms while keeping every required action available.
4. Use familiar controls and predictable feedback so older and occasional users do not need training.
5. Let real data and completion state carry meaning; keep interface chrome restrained.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for product surfaces and at least 7:1 contrast in presentation mode. Team Manager mobile workflows require 44 by 44 pixel minimum touch targets, readable text without iOS input zoom, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Responsive layouts must remain usable from 320 pixel phones through desktop and support both touch and pointer input.
